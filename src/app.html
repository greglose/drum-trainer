Pad map

Click a pad, then hit it on the kit to learn its MIDI note. Defaults are the Alesis Nitro factory map. Hi-hat open vs closed: if the module sends different note numbers (46 open, 42 closed), those are used. If it sends one note for both, learn both "Hi-hat closed" and "Hi-hat open" to that same note and the pedal position (controller 4) decides instead: pressed = closed.

No kit handy? Keyboard: space kick, J snare, F closed hat, G open hat, D foot hat, R T Y toms, U crash, I ride.
